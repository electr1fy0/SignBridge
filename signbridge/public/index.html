        Calibration instructions appear here.

        Calibration runs at startup and records the straight-hand flex readings and the still gyro. Re-run it whenever the glove is put on again: straighten your fingers and hold your hand flat and still. Serial output is 115200 baud.

        Bend triggers

        The bar is each finger’s live change from calibration; drag the handle to where it should count as bent. Lower triggers more easily. A finger reads straight again below two thirds of its trigger.
