        Calibration instructions appear here.

        Calibration runs at startup and records the straight-hand flex readings and the still gyro. Re-run it whenever the glove is put on again: straighten your fingers and hold your hand flat, palm down, and still (palm-up combos are measured from this pose). Serial output is 115200 baud.

        Learn hand shapes

        Hand shapes use only the thumb, ring and pinky sensors. Three joins learned matching once it has a recording. Record each shape up to three times: press Record, make the shape during the 3-second countdown, and hold it for 2 seconds. Vary it slightly between recordings the way you naturally would. Once every shape has at least one recording, the glove matches your hand against these recordings instead of the bend triggers. Weak sensors still help as long as they change the same way each time. Recalibrating keeps the recordings.

          Learn motions

          Record each motion up to three times: press Record, hold still, and when the glove shows MOVE NOW do the motion once and stop. Once every motion has at least one recording, the glove matches movements against your recordings instead of the built-in rules; movements that match nothing well are ignored. More recordings make it more forgiving.

            Bend triggers

            Used for hand shapes until all four are recorded above. The bar is each finger’s live change from calibration; drag the handle to where it should count as bent. Lower triggers more easily. A finger reads straight again below two thirds of its trigger.
